Version 1.3.1 improves telemetry accuracy and reliability:

• battery state of charge and voltage now come from the IBS/battery ECU;
• regeneration-finish alerts no longer require observing an exact zero-distance sample;
• regeneration history recovers when DPF load is missing from the first active sample;
• CarPlay telemetry refreshes follow Apple’s 10-second limit;
• the CarPlay alert bell now shows and confirms its state more clearly;
• improved ECU-header discovery and handling of temporarily unavailable data.
