Version 1.2 improves reliability, Live Activities and available data:

• new native CarPlay dashboard with direct adapter connection, updated DPF data and regeneration notifications;
• redesigned Live Activity without the double border, fully localised in Italian and English;
• more reliable DPF polling, protected from interfering diagnostic requests;
• detection of Konnwei KW903 Bluetooth LE adapters and support for the FFE0/FFE1 profile;
• smoother dashboard and more readable value transitions;
• removed unreliable automatic vehicle-model identification;
• clearer timeouts and messages when an adapter cannot be found or is incompatible;
• tapping the Live Activity now opens the app and reconnects correctly;
• screen wake behaviour fixed during interruptions and reconnections;
• added an optional project-support link in Settings; the app remains completely free.
