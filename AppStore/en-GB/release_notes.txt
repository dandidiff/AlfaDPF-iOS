Version 1.3 makes DPF monitoring clearer, more accessible and more reliable:

• support for Bluetooth LE and Wi-Fi TCP ELM327 adapters;
• matching DPF load thresholds across the iPhone and CarPlay dashboards;
• a prominent connection control and confirmation before disconnecting during a regeneration;
• an indicative remaining-time estimate when enough progress data is available;
• history insights for average duration, load reduction and completion rate, plus an interactive chart;
• a more adaptive interface with Dynamic Type, VoiceOver and Reduce Motion support;
• in-app language selection for Italian, English, French and Spanish;
• more robust handling when a PID becomes temporarily unavailable.
