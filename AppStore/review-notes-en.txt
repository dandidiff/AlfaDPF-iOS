Alpha DPF Monitor does not require an account or login.

The production connection requires:
- a compatible ELM327 adapter using Bluetooth Low Energy;
- a supported Alfa Romeo/FCA diesel vehicle;
- ignition on and the vehicle safely parked.

The complete UI can be reviewed without external hardware:
1. Launch the app.
2. Tap the test-tube icon in the top bar.
3. Tap “Run full cycle (8 seconds)” (the Italian UI label is “Esegui ciclo completo (8 secondi)”).
4. The app cycles through clean filter, high load, regeneration start, regeneration at 52%, and regeneration completion.
5. If prompted, allow notifications to review the local start/end alerts and Live Activity.

Notifications are localized (Italian and English): titles and bodies for
regeneration start/finish and the test alert are translated, including the
formatted DPF load and duration values.

CarPlay:
- Apple approved the app for the CarPlay Driving Task category. The app uses the `com.apple.developer.carplay-driving-task` entitlement.
- The native CarPlay scene uses a `CPGridTemplate` dashboard and a second-level `CPInformationTemplate`; it does not claim navigation, audio, charging, parking, or communication functionality.
- Connect CarPlay and open Alpha DPF Monitor. The compact Connect/Cancel/Disconnect control in the navigation bar starts or stops the same BLE/ELM session used by the iPhone app; the CarPlay scene never creates a second adapter connection or diagnostic poller.
- The dashboard shows only DPF load, distance since regeneration, exhaust temperature, regeneration progress/count/status, categorical oil-pressure status, battery voltage, and the last-update time. Missing or cached values are explicitly identified and never fabricated.
- Periodic CarPlay data-item updates are limited to once every 10 seconds, as required by Apple's Driving Task guidelines. Connection-button state changes update immediately and do not trigger additional OBD requests.
- On iOS 18.4 and later, regeneration start/finish notifications use a notification category with Allow in CarPlay. On earlier supported iOS versions, or when system CarPlay notifications are disabled, a `CPAlertTemplate` is used as a non-duplicating in-app fallback.
- The bell button shows the current CarPlay notification settings, an immediate in-app `CPAlertTemplate` test, and a system-notification test delayed by 10 seconds so the reviewer can return to CarPlay Home before delivery.
- The CarPlay UI can be opened without OBD hardware to review the disconnected/cached state. Live values require the compatible adapter and vehicle listed above.

The waveform icon opens a local diagnostic log. The info icon opens the safety notice, privacy policy, and support links.

- The contribution unlocks no content, functionality, status, or service. The app remains fully functional and free whether or not the user contributes.
- No payment SDK is embedded in the app; the HTTPS link opens the external Ko-fi page.

The app does not collect data, use analytics or advertising, create user accounts, or send vehicle data to a server. Bluetooth is used only to communicate directly with the selected adapter.

Safety: configure and use the app only while the vehicle is stationary. The app is informational and does not replace professional diagnostics or vehicle maintenance.
